# Short Stories:

18:30 UTC, Ewigkeit, just outside the J-31 Solar System. Elios was in his quarters getting prepared to land at Neu-Preussen with the General secretary for a state visit. They were there to discuss the recent crackdowns on 'bourgeois' culture. He asked personally to join the discussion as he thought about his brother who ran an underground club in Kareuth. 

18:50 UTC, He leaves his quarters and heads for the teleportation room where Kael was talking to the engineers about the landings. He stopped just at the corridor before straightening up his tunic. 

*Okay Elios, you got this. He's a reasonable man, and you're his second in command. He'll grant your wish* 

Kael notices Elios and greets him in K'tharrian, "Elios. Golukta Sirosi. Are you ready for our state visit?"

"Y-yes sir." He replied. 

"Good." Kael glanced at his wrist, "We depart in a minute."

"O-oh uhm. Sir?"

"Hm?"

"Sir, I know it's out of protocol and this kind of thing is against state policy, but do you think we can make a short trip to my brother in his club?" Elios gulped, almost choking on his words. 

"Your brother runs a club?" Kael said raising an eyebrow.

"Y-yes sir. But I promise you, it's nothing out of the ordinary. Follows rules. It's registered. Just a few drinks and rock music s-sir."

Kael stood there for a second, contemplating the request. "Alright. You have thirty minutes."

"R-Really sir?" Elios' eyes sparkled as the general secretary approved of his request. 

"Thirty minutes. Not one more."

"T-thank you sir. You won't regret this!"

Kael gave him a single nod and turned back to the teleport pad. Elios followed, and as the light rose around them, his relief gave way to a smaller, colder thought.

_Please let the place be in order._

19:05 UTC, just outside the club, it was booming with music as bright notes in odd-time signatures played. 

"This is the place sir." 

"Looks.. well kept."

"Yes sir! I monitor my brother every week."

Kael said nothing, just glanced at his wrist and walked in.

Elios peeled backstage towards the manager's room to meet up with his brother. Two K'tharr body guards guarded the door. They looked like warriors straight from the homeland.

"Name and purpose?" asked the guard. 

"My name is Elios, Elios Kashrak. Second in command, and brother of your boss." Elios crossed his arms, squaring his shoulders.

"I don't believe it, show me your ID."

Elios pulled out his military ID and gave it to the guards. The looks on their faces as they realized they'd almost defied the second most powerful man in the galaxy was priceless.

"S-sorry sir. Head right on in sir." the two stepped aside.

"Good." Elios walked in and met his brother sitting at his table. 

"Elli! Long time no see. To what do I owe this visit to." Ju'un smiled at Elios, one of his teeth golden.

"Hello Ju'un. Just visiting, seeing if your place is following strict conduct rules." he glanced at the registration certificate, the date was recent, "Wouldn't want my brother in a reeducation camp."

"Eh, no worries bro. I've been following rules to the tee. Everything's in order! Though it'd be nice if your folks would announce their visits."

"Wouldn't be a random visit if they'd announce it Ju'un, you know that."

"Eh, whatever. I keep the place clean anyways. They got nothin on me baby."

"Sure. How's mom?"

"She's good. That new delivery of med tech from Terra saved her life."

"And dad?"

"Dead. Good riddance."

His heart sunk at the sudden news.

"Dad's dead? Why didn't I hear of this?"

"Figured you wouldn't wanna be bothered with the news, given that you already have enough on your plate. Bein second in command and what not."

Dad. Wasn't the best guy, in fact he was a terrible father. But he was the one that raised him to be who he was.

"Well in any case, hope you don't take it too hard. I got a lotta paperwork to get through, you guys don't really make it easy for us normal folks to live life."

"Its to keep things in order Ju'un. "

"Sure sure. Hey say hi to the General Secretary for me eh? Tell him the Lowdown club got a seat for him" Ju'un said as Elios left his office. 

Elios, kept his head down as he went back to Kael. He took a deep breath and choked in all his emotions. 

Kael looks up at the Elios, 

"Everything in order commander?"

"Yes sir! My brother is running the place as ordered."

"Good to hear. Let us convene with the central committee then."

"Lets." 

 # Short Story 2: 

14:10 UTC, Berlin Palace, Terra. Elios sat at a desk buried in trial papers, going through every signature, receipt and pamphlet again. All of it led to one man, just like Kael said. But a courier receipt kept bugging him. It was countersigned by a woman, Lena Vogt, and her name showed up nowhere else in the case. Not on the pamphlets, not on the maps. A name that clean in a file that tidy was worth following.

_Let's see where you go._

15:00 UTC, Berlin, Terra. The streets were packed with humans, K'tharrians and Vorax. Vaen flew overhead. Elios was wearing regular clothes, a long coat hiding his tail, as he tailed Lena through the crowd. He weaved between the shoppers, eyeing down his mark. She took a left turn, then another, then another.

_Shit. She knows._

Lena looked back at Elios before crossing the street. A car stopped and picked her up. Elios took note of the plate number.

_They're onto me. Better be careful._

08:00 UTC the next morning, Berlin People's Police Department.

"Are you sure it's not in the records?" Elios asked.

"Sorry sir. We can't find the plate number."

"Fuck. They were using a fake one."

"Well, that adds to their list of crimes beside hate crimes." the cop joked, slapping his knees.

Elios gave him a death glare and the cop straightened up.

"Uhh, okay well maybe we can trace where the fake plate came from."

"Really?"

"Yeah, we know a couple of guys who make them in the district. They might know a thing or two."

"Oh good. Give me their names. Now."

"Yeesh, who woke up on the wrong side of the bed." the cop retorted before printing a file and handing it to Elios.

"There, you got their addresses and codes too. These people are wary of military men by the way, so you'd probably want to lose the get up."

"Of course."

"Good luck sir."

"Thank you."

Elios looked down at the file as he walked out. Names, addresses, codes, all of it. Too easy.

13:30 UTC, East Berlin. It was a rundown car shop, no, chop shop. The gate was rusty, you could get tetanus just looking at it. Elios had left his military uniform and credentials back at the palace and borrowed a leather jacket from one of his junior officers. He knocked on the door beside the gate.

"CAN'T YOU READ THE SIGN, WE'RE CLOSED! FUCK OFF!"

"Yikma Tarusa."

The sliding gate opened. Inside were stacks of fake plates, a rusty old car with no wheels, a printing press and three K'tharrians.

"Waddya want?"

"I need a plate."

The K'tharrian facing him was smaller than usual, maybe 5'5", and looked like the boss of the place.

"Sayyy, haven't I seen you before?"

"What do you mean?"

"On television. Are you an actor or something?"

"No sir."

"Sir.. Are you military?"

Elios' sweat broke at his chin.

"Go fuck yourself, we don't serve your kind here."

"No wait, please. I need your help."

The head K'tharrian crossed his arms and raised his eyebrows.

"Look, extraterrestrial lives are at stake here, and you guys can help me figure out who the ring-leader of the humanist bloc is."

"What's in it for us?"

"I can run favors. I can make sure your business runs without any inspections."

"Deal."

The boss pulled a worn ledger from under the printing press and flipped a few pages. "Black car, plate ending nine-four-one. Paid in cash by a woman. She said to deliver it to a garage near Alexanderplatz. Asked for me by name, but not hers."

"That's it?"

"That's it. You wanted a name, go bug the garage." The boss grinned. "And remember our deal, Mr. Military."

Elios nodded and walked out. Halfway down the street he stopped.

_Inspectors with no personal stakes._ Kael had said it to Noel in front of the whole committee. And here he was, selling exemptions out of a borrowed jacket.

He kept walking.

15:30, Alexanderplatz, Berlin. Elios found the garage, squat concrete building. A noodle shop beside it and a boarded up bookstore on the other side. "Chester's auto" the sign said. _Let's go in then_

He meets a mechanic working on a sports car, his overalls stained with oil and other kind of gunk. 

"Hello there sir. How may I help you?"

"You Chester?"

"Yep thats me!.. Waaaiiit a minute.. Are you a cop?"

"No sir."

"Military."

_Goddamnit._ 

Chester squints scanning Elios' face.

"Oh you're that pushover second-in-command to that bitch Kael." his face dropping to a frown. "What do you need? Information? Cause I ain't got any"

Elios drops down his tail, balling up his fist. He was holding back the urge to beat the brakes off this guy. 

"Listen, I just need a name."

"Nope. Don't talk to cops."

"Listen sir, lives are at stake here."

"Lives are at stake," Chester repeated in a mocking voice. "Like you actually care about regular folks. You throw em in labor camps willy nilly and you come to me about 'lives are at stake'."

"You don't understand."

"No you listen here boy. I understand perfectly. You prance around in your little itty palace, while the rest of us folks down here do the dirty work. So don't lecture me about not understanding."

Elios looked down at the ground, his ears steaming. He takes a deep breath, it didn't help. His mouth and body moved before his brain did.

"Alright, you give me no choice."

He grabs the man by the collar and slams him to the wall.

"HEY WHAT THE FU-" the man exclaimed before being coverd by Elios' hand.

"Here's whats going to happen. You give me names, and I won't convene a tribunal for high treason and condemn your ass to causal erasure. Got that?"

Chester nods. Elios uncovers his mouth.

"Ok, ok, ok. Look, you're looking for a black car right? They come in every month. Changes plates and paint. Nothing less, nothing more. Man pays in cash everytime. He slipped up once. Coat fell open and he had a badge on his belt. Police. Berlin, maybe. I didn’t get a good look."

"Police?" He let's go of the mechanic to fall to the floor.

_Of course it is_

Elios turns and leaves withour saying one last word. 

"YOU'RE WELCOME." Chester shouted. 

# Short Story 2, Part 2:

Elios walked into the noodle shop across the street and ordered a coffee. He needed a break.

“Smoking allowed here?”

“No one else here, sir. Go ahead.”

He took out a cigarette with a red tip, lit it, and inhaled slowly. Then he pulled the book Kael had assigned him from his coat pocket and cracked it open with a wince, taking another drag. Chapter II: Basic Problems of the Filipino People. It described a country that was semi-colonial and semi-feudal, held for years under imperialist America. He tried to read it intently and caught himself skimming. He would have to read it again with fresh eyes.

When he looked up, a black sedan was parking outside the boarded-up bookshop. He set the cigarette in the ashtray. Two men got out in trench coats, scanned the street, and let themselves in. The lights stayed off.

They came out a few minutes later carrying boxes stamped MARXIST LITERATURE. Elios looked at the book in his hand, then at the boxes.

He thought about going after them. But he was alone, with no backup, and the only people he could call were Berlin’s police, who had handed him a file a little too easily. So he took out a small camera, got the plate, the faces, and the boxes, and let the sedan go.

08:00 UTC the next morning, Elios’s office, Berlin Palace. He had not used the holoboard. Every query on the palace network was logged, and he no longer knew who read the logs. Instead he had pinned everything to a corkboard with red string, a human affectation he’d picked up from old detective vids. It helped him think. The courier receipt, the fake plate, the cop’s file, the bookshop. Every string ended in the same circle.

_Too fucking clean._

He stared at it for a long time. Then he stopped looking at the board and went through who had known what, and when. Lena had spotted his tail within minutes of his starting it. The police had printed him a file before he finished asking. Both had been a step ahead of him, as if someone had told them he was coming.

He went back to the beginning. He had taken Lena’s name to exactly one person at People’s Intelligence, and Ashe had printed the address before he’d finished asking.

His stomach turned. _No._ Ashe was Kael’s husband. Ashe had made him laugh at his own tunic.

But Ashe had also sat at that desk and been the first to see the file. If anyone had watched her open it...

_Ashe, what did you do?_


# Short Story 2, Part 3: 

19:40 UTC, Hellersdorf, Berlin, Terra. Elios moved through the shadows on a rented bike, keeping two bikes back from Otto Muller, the cop who had given him the file a little too fast. He weaved through the bike paths and the other riders, noting every turn. He wanted to wring the truth out of the man, but he had promised himself restraint tonight.

20:00 UTC, Otto’s apartment building. Elios watched Otto walk inside and waited a couple of beats before following. The two guards at the entrance, one human and one Vaen, stopped him.

“Visiting or inquiring?”

“Investigating. Commander Elios Kashrak. Now move aside.” He produced his military credentials, and the guards complied.

“Where is Otto Muller’s room?”

“Seventh floor, room 713, sir,” the human guard said with a straight face.

“Thanks. And boys? I want those logs. I was never here alright?”

"Y-yes sir." the guards handed Elios the logbook. Elios pocketed it and intended to burn it later.

Elios takes the elevator to the seventh floor. A sign hanging on the wall said that rooms 710-720 were at the right corridor. He heads right and looked at the door numbers, 710, 711, 712, 713. _Gotcha._

Elios knocks on the door, he looks at his holstered side piece.

“Hold on, hold on, I’m coming.” Otto opened the door, and his eyes widened at the sight of Elios. His smile came a second too late. “Oh, if it isn’t Mister Wrong-Side-of-the-Bed. First question. How’d you find me? Second question. Why'd the guards let you in? Third question-” Elios cut him off.

“None of your business, Otto. May I come in?”

“Do I have a choice?”

“Either you let me in, or I shove my pistol down your throat. I’m willing to bet your insides don’t react well to chaos energy pellets.”

The words were out before he had decided to say them. _Restraint. You lasted twenty minutes._ He kept his hand off the holster and did not look at Otto, because he did not want to see whether the man believed him.

Otto stepped aside, and Elios walked in. The apartment was cluttered, with unwashed dishes piled in the sink and a full trash can. Elios looked around in disgust.

“So, Otto. The addresses, the names, the plate. You told me to go without my uniform. You gave me the information a little too quickly.”

“Well, I figured you were higher up and you needed it fast...”

“We have systems in place, Otto. Information isn’t handed out just like that. It goes up a chain of command for approval.”

Otto sweated. “Well, what can I say, haha. I work fast?”

Elios balled his fist. He couldn’t hold it in. The bastard cop was dodging him. He pulled out his pistol, walked up to Otto, and grabbed him by the collar. He pressed the barrel into the man’s throat and kept his finger on the trigger. _Damn it, Elios. Keep it together._

“Start talking.” His mouth had betrayed him again.

“WHOA, ALRIGHT, ALRIGHT. Listen, someone said you were coming, alright? I had the file ready immediately. I thought it was a test or a pre-instruction or something. I didn’t do nothing.”

“Who told you?”

“I- I don’t know.” Otto looked away, guilty.

Elios aimed the pistol at a glass on the table and pulled the trigger without looking. The tip glowed red as the pistol hummed to life, and the shot turned the glass to fine ash, leaving a grey smear on the tabletop.

“Talk.”

“I SWEAR I DON’T HAVE A NAME. They called on an i-internal line... oh-forty, it said on the screen. I swear I don’t know who sent it. Please don’t shoot me.”

“Internal line. Who could’ve possibly called you?”

“Could be Compliance... They do that sometimes, you know? To test you and shit...”

Elios let go of the officer and holstered his pistol. Otto clutched his neck as if checking that he was still alive.

Elios moved for the door and stopped at the frame. “Oh, and Otto? You tell anyone I was here, and your family receives your urn, delivered by me personally. Got that?” Once more, his mouth moved before his brain did. He cursed himself for it.

# Shorty story 3: 

10:00 UTC, Mitte, Berlin, Terra. The streets filled with people walking to their offices, cars whizzing past. At the side of the road, an unmarked grey sedan was parked across from the Ministry of Accountancy. Ashe sat in the driver’s seat and Elios was in the passenger seat, smoking his favorite brand of cigarettes, red filters. Of the three departments marked 040, this was the one Elios’s notes tied to the Vogt courier receipt, so this was where they started. Elios stared out the window thinking about the events of last night. _That joke about a K’tharr kid lying to his elders, how did I not spot that._

“Hey commander. Snap out of it.” Ashe said plainly. “I could hear you think, and it’s deafening.”

Elios turned his head towards Ashe with a plain look on his face. “S-sorry. I was just..”

“It’s fine. I know what you’re thinking about.” He put his cup of coffee on the holder, “Lay it out on me.”

“The other night..” he hesitated. “I- I had seen him do it countless times over my six years under him. I’ve dragged men out of the dinner room who were causally erased the day after..” He took a drag off his cigarette. “I’d noticed after the second drink. I kept drinking anyways.”

“If you’d stopped that would’ve been a tell as well.”

“Yeah.. “ his tail curling under his seat.

“I just can’t believe he made me, his partner, sit through that, in my own home... He called me Agent Ashe. He- he. URGHH” Ashe slammed his fists on the steering wheel.

Elios stayed silent.

“The worst part is that I understand why he did it.” A tear dropping down his cheek, “The even worst part is that I would’ve done the same.”

Elios pats his back. Ashe took a deep breath.

“I still love him, but. Now there’s fear in my heart. That night, I didn’t see Kael, I saw the general secretary.”

“Me too.” Elios said.

“It’s going to take a lot out of him to make me lose that fear.” Ashe said, eyeing out the entrance once more.

“There, there she is again.”

“Who?”

“Grey coated woman, I saw her earlier. Looked kinda shifty. Now she came out with a briefcase. Accountancy just went from a guess to our front-runner.”

“Then let’s tail her.” Elios threw out his cigarette.

“Keep your side piece ready commander, this could get ugly.”
