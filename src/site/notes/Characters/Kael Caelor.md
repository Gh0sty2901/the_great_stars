# Short story: 

Kael woke at exactly 04:00 UTC+0 in his apartment in a high-density Soviet bloc in Berlin. He rose in his nightgown and made his bed with care, folding the sheets evenly and arranging the pillows symmetrically at its head.

In the bathroom, he met his own deadpan gaze in the mirror. He removed his nightgown, took the pumice from its place at the edge of the sink, and began shedding his scales. When he finished, he squeezed a small amount of K'tharr Shine'o, a tincture formulated to make K'tharr scales gleam, onto his palms, dabbed it along his cheeks, and rubbed it in. He repeated the same careful dab and scrub across his whole body until his dark red scales shone.

He folded his nightgown and carried it to his walk-in wardrobe, where green military tunics, trousers, and nightgowns hung in labeled rows. He dressed slowly, taking care not to crease the tunic or the trousers.

Next came the tea. He prepared a traditional K'tharr blend through an elaborate ceremony that kept him grounded and disciplined and reminded him of his roots. Cup in hand, he crossed to his library: roughly three hundred books in labeled sections, from historical sources to scientific journals to fiction, ending in a full shelf devoted to Marxism. He drew a volume from that last shelf, settled into the chair at the center of the living room, and set his tea on the table beside him. The book was _Historical Materialism in the Time of Galactic Abundance_. He read it line by line, nodding, jotting notes in his journal.

By 06:00 UTC+0 he had reached page 200, and his tea was finished. Ready to address the galaxy, Kael returned the book to its exact place on the shelf and left for the central committee.

# Short story 2:

18:30 UTC in Kareuth, Neu-Preussen. Work just finished for most people and the streets are only starting to get filled up. Third places start opening their doors in anticipation of visitors looking to shake off the day. One such place was the 'Low-down Rock Club', ran by a K'tharr resident named Ju'un Kashrak, brother of high commander Elios Kashrak, second in command to Kael.

19:00 UTC. The MPS (Milkyway People's Ship) Ewigkeit hovered just outside of the Neu-Preussen solar system. Aboard it was Elios Kashrak, Kael and the rest of the crew. They were here for a state visit. Before teleporting in, Elios approached the General Secretary with a request. 

"Sir, I know it's out of protocol and this kind of thing is against state policy, but do you think we can make a short trip to my brother in his club?"

"Your brother runs a club?"

"Y-yes sir. But I promise you, it's nothing out of the ordinary. Follows rules. It's registered. Just a few drinks and rock music S-sir."

Kael thought about it for a second. He's been running the galaxy non-stop for how many months at this point? Perhaps indulging in some music would be nice. "Alright. You have thirty minutes."

"R-Really sir?"

"Thirty minutes. Not one more."

"T-thank you sir. You won't regret this!"

Elios and Kael teleported in, Kael donning a trench coat over his military clothes and wearing sunglasses so as not to be spotted. They enter the club in the middle of the city and it was booming with math-rock. Odd-time signatures mixed with bright guitar notes. Why this would go perfect with some tea. 

"Alright sir, I'll go talk to my brother. 30 minutes."

"Mhm," Kael said as he eyed the bar. He went over and ordered a cup of K'tharr tea. He takes a sip of it and winces. Barkeep probably just heard of tea. 

He went to a stool, just nearby the exit where he can see it. He placed his cup of tea square on the coaster. He nodded along to the music, tapping his boot on the stool. He's always had an inclination towards human music. It was a guilty pleasure.

A young human in a grey hoodie approached him, seemingly nervous, almost excitedly so. *Uh-oh, someone spotted me.* 

"Hey man. Aren't you general secretary Kael?"

"An astute observation young man. Who are you?"

"M-my name's Yeshua and my friend over there is a big fan of yours." pointing at the hybrid reptile by the bar. 

*a hybrid* Kael thought, taking note of the young man's features. 

"His name is Ja'ax. He's a pretty cool dude, got good grades."

"Ja'ax, sounds familiar. I believe I've read about him in a couple of reports from your school." He stared at the obviously tipsy young human up and down as he bobbled while he stood, "You on the other hand, you need to watch yourself."

He stood up from his stool to greet the hybrid. 

"Ja'ax. Golukta Sirosi. Glad to see the Neu-Preussen project has worked wonders." he said in K'tharrian.

The hybrid faced him. Kael observed Ja'ax face, he's nervous, scales rigid. He knew exactly who he was. He stepped a pace back not to scare him. 

"Your friend here told me about you. I've read your name in some interesting reports from your school as well."

"W-what, R-really?"

"Correct. If you keep it up, you might be able to work at sector -200. You'd make a great scientist there."

"T-thank you s-sir."

"Well you boys stay out of trouble now. I like what I'm seeing here so I'll send a notice to the Neu-preussen Central committee that this location is a credit to the district." Kael said in German once more and with a nod before heading back to his stool to finish his tea. He looks at the time, it's been 25 minutes. 

Elios then appears from behind the stage and approaches the general secretary. 

"Everything in order commander?"

"Yes sir! My brother is running the place as ordered."

"Good to hear. Let us convene with the central committee then."

"Lets." 

# Short story 3: 

20:00 UTC. The streets were cleared, autonomous road-cars still ran in the streets though with no one riding. Though there wasn't any official curfew, everyone was either home or at a third place. The walk to the People's palace in the center of the Neu-Preussen capital Kareuth was serene. 

"Commander." called out Kael to Elios as his mind looked like it wandered off. 

"Yes sir!" Elios snapped to attention.

"Lower your voice commander, it is night time."

"S-sorry Sir. You just caught me.. thinking."

"About your father I presume?"

"Sir?"

"Oh yes, I heard about it in a report just a few days ago. My condolences."

Elios' face stooped down, upset.

"Commander."

"Yes Sir.."

"Have I ever told you I used to be a K'tharr warrior before being picked up by the Terrans."

"No Sir."

"I was raised communally, raised to be a great warrior. That meant I never got to know my father nor my mother."

"..."

"After the 7 hours war, I was placed in a reeducation camp put up by that tyrant Georg where they taught me everything I knew. Until then at least."

"S-sir why are you telling me this?"

"My point is, you're lucky to have met your father. I was raised by anyone but my blood relations. First by warriors, then by revisionists, and finally by the blinding light of Marxism."

".."

Kael stopped, his hands behind his back. Elios stopped as well looking at his superior. 

"Think of it this way Elios, you may have lost your father. But you still have the Ewigkeit crew and me behind you."

Elios eyes' lit up once more. "T-thank you Sir."

"Now now, no need to be so jovial. We've got a score to settle with the central committee."

"Yes Sir!"

"Modulation Elios."

"Sorry again Sir."

# Short Story 4:

19:00 UTC, aboard the Ewigkeit floating through the Milkyway Galaxy. The corridors were quiet, only clanking with the sound of boots stomping on the catwalks. Except for one corridor deep in the Ewigkeit, the General Secretary's quarters. It was booming with sounds of deep connection, every scream, every name call, followed by the sound of scale and skin slapping and scratching each other. 

The two bodyguards guarding the door looked down at the floor as they heard how loud the two were getting in there. 

"I don't get paid enough for this crap." said Robin, a human guard.

"You realize we don't get paid at all? It's a post scarcity society." the other K'tharrian guard retorted. The two in the bedroom, getting louder every slap.

"Boy they're really getting it on in there huh."

"Kael's definitely destroying his ass that's for sure."

"How long does this usually take."

"About 30 minutes."

"30 MINUTES???" the human exclaimed realizing he was getting loud and lowered his voice. "30 minutes?? How virile is our General Secretary man."

"Very."

"I regret my job choices."

"Same."

The connection stopped with one last slap and shouting of names. 

"Oh? Looks like they just finished."

"God I fucking hope so."

The sliding door opened, the General Secretary and his human husband came out the door. "Appreciate the lookout, Robin, Ilyan. Priority access to material synthesizers for one month for the both of you." Kael said as his husband leaned on him, waddling. 

"Hun, I'm hungry."

"After how much I fed you? You're insatiable." Kael smirked.

The couple's voices echoed as they walked away from their quarters.

"You know what. Worth it." the human said.
