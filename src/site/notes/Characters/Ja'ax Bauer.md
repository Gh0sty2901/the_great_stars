# Short Story: 
The city of Kareuth was bustling with life yet quiet. Unlike on Terran, there was a distinct lack of private motor vehicles. Instead, the inhabitants of Neu-Preussen rode bikes, fusion-cell powered busses, road-cars and walked to their destinations. You could get from one end of the city to the other in only 15 minutes without owning a private vehicle. People mixed freely in the city. Cafe tables were full of people who didn't know each other, people greet each other, and cultural performers lingered in the streets. The people were happy, and it shows, humans, hybrids and k'tharrians waved and smiled at each other and congregated around performers.

One such inhabitant of Kareuth was Ja'ax, a human-k'tharr hybrid. He looked almost human, but his skin was rougher and tougher than normal, with a faint, scaly texture you could feel if you brushed his arm; he had his mother's eyes, reptilian eyes, and a tail. He walked by a performer, not giving them a single look. He was headed to the Kareuth institute of Technologies, on his way to take an exam on quantum algorithms. His back was slouched, deadpan gaze, centuries old songs played by a band called "Nirvana" boomed in his earphones.

He reached the front yard of the campus, where the smell of fresh food hit the air as students gathered around picnics and conversed at table lounges. He glanced at an erect statue of a scientist holding a hammer and a sickle, something he'd been told was supposed to mean the "proletarianisation of the sciences"; something he still didn't get. He headed straight indoors and to his locker to grab his book on quantum computing, since he had a lot more to review that day. Beside his locker, he was greeted by Yeshua, an American human straight from Terra, the home planet of humans.

"Yooo big J, my cold blooded brother, what's up my man," Yeshua called out, his voice too loud this early in the morning. Ja'ax scales flicked at the high energy. Yeshua then reached out for a dap to the reptile hybrid

"What up Yesh. You seem mighty jovial today, what's new with you?" Ja'ax dapped up the human, making a sharp pop sound. Ja'ax started walking towards his classroom, Yeshua followed behind.

"You know what it is big boy! Party last night was off the chains, aha. We was drinking til the end of the night!" Yeshua grinned, his words making Ja'ax's scales twitch with irritation.

"Dude you know we're not supposed to drink on a school night. You know if they catch you hung over, its your ass. Plus we have an exam today." Ja'ax shot back, flicking his tail in annoyance. "You know they've been cracking down on 'bourgeois culture' in Kareuth. The Central Committee announced that last week".

"Nah bruh, its fine." Yeshua snickered, wiping his nose. Ja'ax rolling his eyes at his bum of a friend "I got this special formulation from Doc Jack that clears your head up immediately."

"What about the exam? Did YOU even review for it?"

"I'll just bum off you man!"

"Tch, sure whatever. Just don't make it too obvious this time around.", Ja'ax didn't care much for his course either, but he'd be damned if he failed anything.

"Lighten up scalie boy."

"Don't call me that," he snapped, his tail flicking from side to side, annoyed even more than before.

"I'm sure I'll be fine since I got good scores on my other shit. Though I really gotta lock in for that Proletarian culture class. I never really got that"

"It's pretty easy, just don't be a bum, like you." Ja'ax jeered.

"Not cool man. I live life like I'm going to die tomorrow, You know humans used to party hard centuries ago".

"Yeah and that's centuries ago, today is today. Times change and you need to get your act up bro."

"You're no fun."

"You say that now, but if you don't get your shit together, they're gonna get your ass." Ja'ax stops in his tracks. "Remember Tyler bro? Man slacked around and he disappeared for two weeks. When he came back he was moving around like he got programmed or something."

Yeshua stopped, gulping while a sweat broke at his chin. "I guess you're right. Alright fine, this is the last time I'll bum off you for an exam."

"Sure"

"I'm serious!"

"Alright, I believe you"

# Short story 2:

19:00 UTC in Kareuth, Neu-Preussen. People rushed home as bikes clacked at the streets, chatter filled the air in the walkway, and full busses and road-cars whizzed by. At this time of day, speakeasies and state controlled bars filled up as people looked to relieve some stress and let loose. Third places like these were popular around Neu-Preussen, both underground and state-controlled. Some underground places got raided by state forces if they were deemed 'bourgeois' but these were pretty rare. In response to a wave of protests, the central committee allowed the existence of these underground locations as long as they registered their locations for occasional monitoring. Some complied, some didn't.

One such underground place was called the "Lowdown rock club", which Ja'ax and his friends frequented. Sometimes they're there to play and cover old grunge songs, other times they'd just listen to the current bands which ranged from soft-rock to emo to metal. Currently on stage was a band called "Just minds", which played twinkly math-rock songs. Bright guitar notes and a shifting rhythm had people nodding along in their seats.  

Ja'ax, sat at a bar stool, tapping along his glass of soda to the rhythm of the band's new single "The Great Stars". Yeshua tapped his shoulder and pointed to a guy wearing a trench coat and sunglasses. 

"Yo. Yo, dude. Look at trench coat over there. Ain't that literally general secretary Kael of the Galaxy slumming it with the regulars as if it isn't a big deal?"

Ja'ax shifted his look over to the trench coated person. Dark red scales gleamed through his wrists and collar, one boot tapping to the beat of the song. Ja'ax only knew one person who had scales that color. He spat out his soda. 

"Holy shit it is. Dark red scales, can't miss it. What's he doing here? Didn't he take command of the Ewigkeit?"

"I thought that guy was a shut in." retorted Yeshua.

"Rarely seen in public, yeah. Which is weird considering that he's in a club like this." he said, thinking about how a man who supposedly signed off on crackdowns on 'bourgeois' culture would hang out in a spot like this.

"Let's go talk to him." Yeshua stood up from his stool before Ja'ax grabbed his sleeve.

"Are you stupid? He's probably just here to relax. He has enough on his plate, keeping the galaxy from falling apart and with whatever nightmare is happening at the Frayed Zone. He does NOT need to deal with your dumbass." his tail flicking in annoyance.

"Ah come on man, I'll just say hi"

"Yesh.."

"Just a hi and what's up!" Yeshua shook off the reptile hybrid and crossed the room straight for the General Secretary.

*We're fucked.* Ja'ax thought.

He watched Yeshua lean toward the general secretary and point back at him. Kael turned his head and lowered his sunglasses an inch. Ja'ax spun his stool to face the bar, which didn't help. The stool scraped. Footsteps came closer. He stared hard at his soda and felt his scales go rigid.

"Ja'ax. Golukta sirosi." Kael addressed him in K'tharrian. "Glad to see the Neu-Preussen project has worked wonders." He hadn't heard anyone speak his language other than his mother.

Ja'ax turned around to face the towering reptile. He was standing a pace away from him.

"Your friend here told me about you. I've read your name in some interesting reports from your school as well."

"W-what? R-really?"

"Correct. If you keep it up, you might be able to work at sector -200. You'd make a great scientist there."

"T-thank you s-sir."

"Well you boys stay out of trouble now. I like what I'm seeing here so I'll send a notice to the Neu-preussen Central committee that this location is a credit to the district." Kael said to them in German once more with a nod before going back to his stool.

Ja'ax clutched his shirt, his heart beating hard and loud. "Holy shit, dude." His scales fluttered in shock.

"See I told you he wouldn't bite!" Yeshua downed another shot as if he'd seen a ghost.

# Short Story 3: 

13:30 UTC, Kareuth Institute of Technologies. The front yard and the student lounges cleared as classes resumed. Birds flew and chirped, and indigenous cicadas hummed in the background as Ja'ax tried to take his exam. Right next to him was Yeshua, copying off him, typical. He tried to ignore it as best as he could and just answer his exam to the best of his knowledge. 

14:30 UTC, class bell rang. The corridors filled with students as they either rushed to get to their next class or get the best spots to hang around. Yeshua and Ja'ax, done with classes for the day, left early. They decided to go to one of their favorite hang out spots at the nearby park where kids went to play and joggers well, jogged. 

15:00 UTC, Kareuth Nature Park. Ja'ax and Yeshua walked side by side, breathing in the fresh air as birds flew overhead. The leaves of the trees were orange and falling, it was Autumn in Neu-Preussen. They sat at a bench at the middle of the park, overlooking a giant statue of Marx and Engels, two guys he'd been taught since he could remember. 

"This is nice." Yeshua noted, swaying his legs over the bench.

"Yeah."

"Do you think people think we're gay?" 

Ja'ax recoiled at the sudden question. 

"Dude what?"

"I'm just saying, we're always together, and people like to talk!"

"If we were gay, that wouldn't be their problem now would it?" Ja'ax said, his scales flicking in embarrassment.

"I'm just saying man, we're basically a gay couple if you think about it."

"Do YOU want to be a gay couple?"

"No," Yeshua said, looking away. Ja'ax could just about notice a faint blush. He wanted to push further but decided not to.

"Whatever man. Let's just enjoy the park," Yeshua muttered as he stared at the statue of Marx and Engels close together.
